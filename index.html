About Me

Hey, I'm Jude Nibbelink, a junior studying Software Design at EVIT. I got into programming through a love of games and quickly fell in love with the craft of developing the code behind all the flashy lights and juicy effects you see in the new hit games.

Right now I'm focused on game development. I believe code should be simple, products should be high-quality, and designs should respect the user's time and attention.

Outside of school I hang out with my friends over video games, play some gnarly lead guitar, and serve at my local church.
